What the agent heard

A support agent never reads what a caller said. It reads what a speech recogniser heard. These are real trials from a benchmark that puts a simulated phone line between the customer and the agent, then grades the agent on what the customer actually asked for.

caller text→edge-tts, en-IN voice→300–3400 Hz · 8 kHz · μ-law→Whisper large-v3-turbo→agent

A missing email gets asked for. A wrong one gets trusted.

Every task opens with the caller giving their email. Sorting trials by what the line did to it:

When the email is gone, the agent notices it has nothing to look up and asks. When Whisper hears a plausible wrong address, the lookup fails and the agent treats that as a wrong email and hands the call to a human. The dropped row includes a price-dispute task whose failures happen after the email was recovered. unknown_email, whose caller gives a genuinely wrong address, is left out. These are counts from 65 of 100 trials banked so far, not a score: the full run is compared against a text run on the same model when both finish.

gradedrule 7 amount_stated_first

The line on its own

Before any agent was involved, the twenty scripted opening lines and their follow-ups went down the line once.

0.149word error rate across 40 lines
7 of 21emails survived, counting spoken “at” and “dot” as recoverable
8 of 20openings lost the email completely

The dropped emails are Whisper's, not the audio processing's: the synthesised speech contains them, and Whisper transcribes the email alone, or followed by “thank you”. Both whisper-large-v3 and its turbo variant drop a short fragment at the very end of an utterance, which is how people read out an email on a call.

How it is measured

  • Only the caller's side goes down the line. The agent's replies reach the simulated customer as text, so a simulator mishearing the agent never counts against it.
  • The grader checks the final database, the tool calls, and eleven policy rules, all against what the customer actually wanted. No model grades anything.
  • Addresses are graded as places, so “Road” for “Rd” passes and a misheard city fails. Re-scoring 100 earlier text trials under that rule changed no verdict.
  • The audio on this page is regenerated through the same line from the text the caller said, with each trial's own seed. The transcripts are the ones the agent received during the run.
